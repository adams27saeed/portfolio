# Inbox Management SOP
**Prepared by:** Sa'eed Adams — Operations & Virtual Assistant Support
**Purpose:** Standard operating procedure for managing a client's inbox to reduce response time, prevent dropped tasks, and maintain a clean, actionable inbox at all times.

---

## 1. Objective

Keep the inbox at (or near) zero unread/unactioned items, ensure nothing time-sensitive is missed, and give the client a fast, clear summary of what needs their personal attention versus what's been handled.

---

## 2. Folder / Label Structure

All inboxes get organized into five working categories:

| Label | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Action Required — Client** | Only the client can respond (decisions, approvals, personal replies) |
| **Action Required — VA** | I can handle directly (scheduling, routine replies, data entry) |
| **Waiting on Reply** | Sent, awaiting response from a third party |
| **FYI / Reference** | No action needed, kept for record |
| **Archive** | Fully resolved, filed for search only |

---

## 3. Daily Triage Process

1. **Sweep every new email within 2 hours of arrival** (business hours) — no email sits unread longer than that.
2. **Sort immediately** into one of the five labels above. Nothing stays in the main inbox once reviewed.
3. **Flag urgency** using a simple tag: 🔴 Same-day / 🟡 This week / 🟢 No rush.
4. **Handle everything under "Action Required — VA" the same day** — draft replies, schedule meetings, forward to the right party, update trackers.
5. **Draft replies for "Action Required — Client" items** where possible, so the client only needs to review and hit send — not write from scratch.

---

## 4. Daily Summary to Client

End of each working day, client receives a short summary (Slack, WhatsApp, or email — client's preference):

> **Inbox Summary — [Date]**
> - 🔴 Needs your input today: [list, 1 line each]
> - 🟡 Needs input this week: [list]
> - ✅ Handled today: [count + brief description]
> - ⏳ Waiting on: [list of outstanding third-party replies]

Keeps the client fully aware without needing to open the inbox themselves.

---

## 5. Response-Time Rules

- **Client-facing replies (on client's behalf):** drafted within 4 business hours, sent within 24 hours unless flagged urgent (same-day).
- **Urgent/time-sensitive items:** flagged and actioned within 1 hour of identification.
- **No email older than 48 hours remains unsorted.**

---

## 6. Escalation Logic

Escalate directly to client (bypass normal queue) if an email involves:
- Legal, contractual, or financial commitments
- Anything from a VIP contact list (client provides this on onboarding)
- Anything explicitly marked urgent by the sender
- Anything I'm not confident how to handle — never guess on the client's behalf

---

## 7. Weekly Review

Every Friday:
- Archive everything fully resolved that week
- Review "Waiting on Reply" — follow up on anything outstanding 3+ business days
- Quick audit: is the labeling system still working, or does it need adjusting for this client's specific inbox patterns?

---

## 8. Tools

- Gmail / Outlook native labels & filters (client's existing platform — no new software forced on them)
- Shared tracker (Google Sheet or Notion) logging anything requiring follow-up beyond same-day
- Calendar integration for anything requiring a scheduled response or meeting

---

*This SOP is a template — adapted per client based on inbox volume, industry, and communication preferences during onboarding.*
